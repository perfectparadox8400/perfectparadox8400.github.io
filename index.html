About Us

We are Perfect Paradox 8400, a First Tech Challenge robotics team located in Cedar City, Utah. Our group, ages 12-18, and is dual affilated with the non-profit organizations: Lion's Club, and 4-H. As a team, we design, construct, program, and compete with our robot each season. We also strive to bring our knowledge to our community through service activities, and community outreach events.

2026-2027 Challenge: "Biobuzz"

Last year’s FTC challenge, DECODE℠, was themed around ancient ruins and mysterious texts. Robots scored points by collecting and launching “artifacts” into the classifier, while teams could earn additional points by placing artifacts in the correct order and scoring enough artifacts to unlock bonus scoring opportunities. This year’s challenge, BIOBUZZ℠, shifts the focus to pollination and biodiversity. Robots collect “Pollen” and “Nectar,” place them into Flowers, and launch Pollen into their Hive to score points. Teams must work strategically with their al liance partners to maximize scoring and complete objectives throughout the match.
